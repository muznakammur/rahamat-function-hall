    Our purpose
    No one should have to hold back a celebration because of cost.

    Hall rent is often one of the biggest expenses of any function. We offer this hall to everyone in our community free of charge, so they can spend what they have on what matters most: their guests, their meal, and the day itself.

    i.

    Free of charge

    There is no rent for using the hall. It is our service to the community.

    ii.

    Open to everyone

    Every family, every faith, every kind of function. If you need a space, ask us.

    iii.

    Handled with care

    Every request is handled personally, respectfully and in confidence.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Anything else? Call us and we'll gladly help.

    Is there really no charge for the hall?

    Yes. Rahamat Function Hall is a community service, and anyone can use the hall free of charge, whatever their background or faith.

    What kinds of functions can we hold here?

    All types of functions: weddings, receptions, birthday parties, anniversaries, religious gatherings, community programmes, meetings and more. If you're unsure, just send a request and ask.

    Do I need to create an account?

    No. Pick a date, add your name and phone number, and send your request on WhatsApp or by email.

    Is my date confirmed once I send the request?

    Not yet. Our team will call you back to talk through your function and confirm. Your date is reserved once it's confirmed.

    Can I visit the hall first?

    Of course. Please call ahead during visiting hours so someone can show you around.
